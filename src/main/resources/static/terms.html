Terms and Conditions

DocuSage is a demonstration of retrieval over your documents. These terms keep that honest.

What it is

A document question-answering demo. Answers are passages extracted from your uploads, with citations, or generated text when the Gemini providers are enabled. Answers are not legal, medical, financial, or other professional advice.

Your uploads

Upload only documents you have the right to share. Do not upload confidential material you are not permitted to disclose. Uploads are held in memory only and are cleared on a service restart, but you should still treat anything you upload as visible to the service while it runs.

Availability

The demo is hosted on a free tier that sleeps when idle, so the first load after idle can be slow, and uploads do not persist across restarts. The service is provided as is, with no guarantee of availability or accuracy.

Acceptable use

Do not misuse the service. Automated abuse, attempts to break the service, and uploads of unlawful material are not welcome.

Changes and contact

These terms may be updated from time to time. The version published here is the current one. Questions about them can be sent to the email address on the owner's portfolio contact page.
