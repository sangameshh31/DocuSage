Privacy Policy

DocuSage lets you upload documents and ask questions about them. This page says plainly what happens to what you upload.

What you upload

PDF, plain text, and markdown files up to 25 MB. Each file is split into chunks, turned into embeddings, and indexed so questions can be answered with citations.

Where it is stored

Uploads, chunks, and embeddings are held in an embedded in-memory database on the server. They are not written to disk, and they do not survive a service restart. The hosted demo sleeps when idle, so a restart clears everything you uploaded.

Deletion

You can delete a document and all of its chunks at any time, using the delete control next to it in the document list.

External AI services

By default, DocuSage runs fully locally: embeddings use feature hashing and answers are extracted passages, so no document text leaves the server. If the Gemini providers are enabled instead, document text is sent to Google's Gemini API and is subject to Google's policies.

What is not collected

DocuSage sets no tracking cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and runs no analytics.

Hosting

The demo is hosted on Render. Render's own policies apply to its hosting infrastructure.
